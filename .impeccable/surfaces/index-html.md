## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is built like a Prairie School building on a Midwestern commercial strip: long horizontal lines, Roman brick, leaded art glass, with the car living inside the architecture. It refuses the category default (dark hero photo of a mechanic, wrench icons, red and blue, a row of service cards) and the cream-paper and sign-painted looks the user rejected.

OWN-WORLD: Umber brick field with raked horizontal joints, warm gray stone ground, sage green field, gold accent, small art-glass squares of ochre, sage and rust. Jost (geometric, 1920s) for headings, Public Sans for text. Lines are long and horizontal and run off the right edge. Square corners. Car details are drawn in the same thin architectural line: a garage-bay elevation with a car in profile, wheel-circle glass motifs, line icons for services.

STORY: A visitor reads the name and promise, taps Call or Directions, notices the 4.8 from 85 reviews plaque, checks hours (TBD) and address, reads why families trust Ted, opens Google reviews, scans services, and ends at a big phone number.

FIRST VIEWPORT: At 390px, umber brick field: tracked gold name with a rule, Jost headline, one-line promise, full-width gold Call button, outlined Directions, then a framed rating plaque (4.8, stars, 85 Google reviews) linking to reviews. Garage-bay elevation drawing and the art-glass band close the hero. A fixed call dock appears once the hero Call scrolls away.

FORM: Prairie School (Chicago-area architecture), position 3 on the re-rolled grounded list; seed:c37356abe3912b39 (reroll 1). User asked to add car-related design and make the rating more visible but not loud.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
