## Scope

`index.html`, the whole one-page spec site for Lupita's Bakery. Mode: Persuade (the visitor decides and acts: call, visit, order a cake).

Primary action: tap to call (847) 419-0970. Then open-now and hours, directions, the featured new item, what they bake, Rosca de Reyes, big orders, social follow links. Proof is the verified address, phone and hours, plus real product names from their own posts; no testimonials or stats exist.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the bakery's wrapping paper and pastry-box kit, drawn in the same red pen as their logo. It refuses the category default of a stock-photo hero, a centered "welcome" headline and a row of identical menu cards, and it must never resemble the builder's earlier "Rótulo" bakery site.

OWN-WORLD: Cream ground, logo red (#B03424), concha pink (#F4C7CF), cocoa (#3B2620). A tiled red line-art pattern (concha, churro, bolillo, cake) behind the hero, the big-orders field and the footer. Chunky rounded Bagel Fat One for display, Nunito Sans for text. Cards have red outlines and a flat pink offset shadow; scalloped doily edges; a pink gift tag for one featured item; a notched ticket for hours; a dashed cut-line label for orders.

STORY: A visitor sees the logo and name, learns if it is open now, taps to call or gets directions, notices the new blueberry lemon loaf on the gift tag, reads the hours ticket, scans what they bake, sees Rosca de Reyes is coming, learns to call for party cakes, and can follow them on Instagram or TikTok.

FIRST VIEWPORT: At 390px, a red bar with the name and an English/Español toggle; below it, on wrapping paper, a cream card with the logo circle, "Lupita's Bakery", "Mexican bakery in Wheeling, IL", the open-now pill, a full-width Call button and a Directions button. A sticky bottom call bar takes over once the hero buttons scroll away.

FORM: Papel de envoltura, direction B, chosen by the user over A ("Trazo", pen line on paper). User's words: "B looks more personal, A looks like claude slop".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the critique, the web-design-guidelines review, fixes, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
