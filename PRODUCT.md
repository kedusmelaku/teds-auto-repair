# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML, CSS and minimal vanilla JS. No framework, no build step. Hosted temporarily on GitHub Pages from `main`.

## Users

Drivers in and around Arlington Heights, IL, almost all on phones, often with a car problem right now: they want to call, find out if the shop is open, and get directions. Many are long-time regulars whose families have used Ted for decades; others are neighbors looking for an honest mechanic. A second audience for this spec version is Ted, who will see it on the builder's phone before a pitch.

## Product Purpose

A spec website for Ted's Auto Repair, which has no website today. Page goals, in order: 1) tap to call, always reachable on mobile; 2) hours (TBD) and directions; 3) why people trust Ted, the review themes in our own words, plus a button to the 4.8-star Google reviews; 4) services as general categories marked TBD; 5) a "Call to schedule" section.

## Positioning

A neighborhood garage that has been around for decades. The claim no competitor can copy: families have trusted Ted for 20 to 30+ years because he tells people what is worth fixing and what is not.

Phone (847) 253-2886 (an older directory number, (847) 577-6909, is not shown; confirm with Ted). ## Capabilities and Constraints

- Review themes (paraphrase only, never quote): honest assessments of what is worth fixing; reasonable labor rates and fair pricing; families using Ted for 20 to 30+ years; minor and major repairs.
- Unknown, must stay visible TBD placeholders: hours, year founded, specific services, makes and models serviced, body work, towing, warranties, appointments vs walk-ins, payment methods, owner photo and story.
- English only for now.
- Unlisted spec site: `noindex, nofollow` meta and a robots.txt that disallows everything.
- AutoRepair JSON-LD with only the verified facts.

## Brand Commitments

- Feel: a trustworthy neighborhood garage that has been around for decades. Plainspoken, solid, easy to read.
- Must not read as cartoonish or resemble an earlier spec site (the user rejected early mockups for that).
- Avoid auto cliches: chrome, flames, racing stripes, carbon fiber, stock photos of mechanics in clean uniforms, blue tech gradients.
- No em dashes in site copy.

## Evidence on Hand

Verified facts above only. The 4.8 rating from 85 reviews is a real accomplishment the user wants visible but not loud. No photos yet: storefront, shop interior and Ted are placeholder frames (TBD). No testimonials may be quoted. No prices, no stats beyond the Google rating and review count.

## Product Principles

1. Calling is the main action and is always within thumb reach.
2. Name, address and phone are unmistakable.
3. Trust comes from what customers say, paraphrased honestly, with the source one tap away.
4. Nothing unconfirmed passes as fact: every unknown is a visible TBD Ted approves.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic HTML, strong contrast for outdoor phone use, alt text slots for every photo, large legible body type for readers of all ages, reduced-motion support.
